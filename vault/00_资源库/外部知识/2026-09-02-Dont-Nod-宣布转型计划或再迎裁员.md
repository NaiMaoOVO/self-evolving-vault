Life is Strange and Aphelion studio Don't Nod probably facing layoffs again, as it announces ominous-sounding "transformation initiative"
"A backdrop of profound changes in the economic conditions of the video game industry."
French game developer and publisher Don't Nod - creator of the Life is Strange series and games like Remember Me, Vampyr, Banishers, Jusant and most recently Aphelion - seems as though it's about to enter another round of layoffs.
In a company press release (via Resetera and translated by DeepL), Don't Nod announced it's "embarking on a transformation initiative" to deal with "profound changes in the economic conditions of the video game industry". Layoffs aren't specifically mentioned, but when referring to a meeting scheduled for Thursday 3rd September, Don't Nod mentioned "a job-preservation plan" and "negotiations with the trade union representing employees", which sounds ominous.
The full announcement reads:
"Independent video game publisher and development studio, announces that it is embarking on a transformation programme.
"Against a backdrop of profound changes in the economic conditions of the video game industry and its funding, coupled with a deterioration in the Group's key economic indicators, the Company considers it necessary to adapt its operating model.
"In this context, the Management has convened the Company’s Employee Representative Bodies for a meeting scheduled for Thursday 3 September 2026. This is the first meeting under the information and consultation procedure with the Social and Economic Committee (CSE) prior to the possible implementation of a job-preservation plan. At the same time, the Management has entered into negotiations with the trade union representing employees within the Company.
