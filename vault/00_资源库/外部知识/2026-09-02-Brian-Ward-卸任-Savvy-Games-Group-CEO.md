# Brian Ward 卸任 Savvy Games Group CEO

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

Brian Ward 已卸任沙特阿拉伯国有游戏公司 Savvy Games Group 的首席执行官一职，该消息源自彭博社获得的一份员工通知。在任期间，他主导了总额达 380 亿美元的投资，涉及多家游戏公司，包括《宝可梦 GO》开发商 Niantic 和《Monopoly Go》开发商 Scopely。此次领导层变动标志着 Savvy Games Group 战略方向的潜在调整，但继任者尚未公布。这一变动发生在公司持续扩大全球游戏产业布局的背景下。

**「背景」** Savvy Games Group 是沙特阿拉伯主权财富基金（PIF）旗下的游戏投资公司，旨在推动沙特游戏产业发展并实现经济多元化。Brian Ward 自 2022 年起担任 CEO，此前曾在动视暴雪等公司任职。他的离职是该公司近期一系列高层变动中的最新一例。

**「影响」** Brian Ward 的离职可能影响 Savvy Games Group 未来的投资策略和运营方向，尤其是在其大规模投资组合的管理上。不过，由于继任者尚未公布，具体影响尚不确定。

## 原文 excerpt

Brian Ward steps down as Savvy Games Group CEO
PIF deputy governor Turqi Alnowaiser to become interim acting CEO, according to document obtained by Bloomberg
Brian Ward is stepping down as CEO of Savvy Games Group, according to a staff note obtained by Bloomberg.
"As Savvy embarks on its next period of transformational growth, this is the right time for new leadership for that evolution," Ward wrote.
Turqi Alnowaiser, deputy governor of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) and head of its International Investments Division, will serve as interim acting CEO.
Ward has served as CEO since Savvy Games Group was established in 2021, overseeing a $37.8 billion investment to expand Saudi Arabia's presence in the global games industry.
Savvy Games Group was set up and is wholly owned by the PIF, which Ward previously described as being part of Saudi Arabia's "economic diversification and social transformation."
During Ward's tenure, Savvy acquired several studios, including Moonton from Bytedance for $6 million earlier this year and Scopely for $4.9 billion in 2023.
Before joining Savvy, Ward held leadership roles at Electronic Arts, Xbox, and Activision.
Ward's decision to leave Savvy came just a month after EA was acquired for $55 billion by a consortium led by the PIF.
Bloomberg reports that this transaction "prompted concern internally at Savvy over how the two giant Saudi-owned video-game businesses will be run, according to people with knowledge of the companies who asked to not be identified."
