# Double Fine 确认从 Xbox 拆分后拥有全部游戏 IP 和发行权

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

Double Fine 确认，在年初与微软 Xbox 拆分后，它拥有（或即将拥有）其在 Xbox 旗下七年期间制作的所有游戏的知识产权和发行权。这一声明意味着该工作室对其作品的控制权将回归，包括未来可能的再版、移植或改编。具体游戏列表和交易条款尚未披露，但此举对工作室的独立运营和粉丝期待有积极影响。

**「背景」** Double Fine 是一家以《脑航员》系列等创意游戏闻名的开发商，于 2019 年被微软收购，成为 Xbox 游戏工作室的一部分。2025 年初，该工作室与微软分道扬镳，重新成为独立工作室。此次确认 IP 和发行权归属，是拆分协议的一部分，确保工作室能保留其创作资产。

**「影响」** 对于 Double Fine 及其粉丝而言，这意味着工作室可以自由决定其游戏库的未来，例如重新发行旧作或开发续作，而不受微软发行策略的限制。不过，具体哪些游戏受影响以及是否有平台独占条款尚不明确。

## 原文 excerpt

Double Fine confirms it owns all its games' IP and publishing rights after Xbox split
Double down.
Double Fine has confirmed it owns – or will shortly own – the intellectual property and publishing rights to every game it made during its seven years under Xbox following the studio's split from Microsoft earlier this year.
CEO Tim Schafer confirmed the news on Kinda Funny Games (thanks, IGN), fielding a fan question about whether buying Double Fine's back catalogue - including Psychonauts 2, Keeper, and Kiln - actually puts money in the studio's pocket.
"I saw a question there: 'Do you guys make the money on games?'" Schafer said. "The transfer of the IP came back to us, and we get the publishing rights back to all our games."
That said, "some of [the games]" remain with Xbox for the time being, but Schafer explained that "it just takes a while for it to all process".
"If you're curious, buying any of our games helps support the studio," he emphasised. "We can turn that money into revenue that helps us extend that runway."
Schafer also revealed Double Fine's next move will be to revive its Amnesia Fortnight game jam as a Kickstarter campaign, letting backers vote on which prototype becomes the studio's next full project. The jam is set to run from 31st August through most of September.
It's the clearest confirmation yet of how Double Fine has come out the other side of Xbox's restructuring. Xbox's wider reorganisation, announced by CEO Asha Sharma in July, affected around 3,200 roles across the division and saw several studios, including Double Fine, spun back out into independence while keeping hold of their IP and back catalogues.
Double Fine itself laid off roughly a quarter of its 90-person team as part of the transition, saying at the time that the cuts were needed to reach "a size that we can sustain."
