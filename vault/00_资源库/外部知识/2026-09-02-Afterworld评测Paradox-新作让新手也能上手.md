# 《Afterworld》评测：Paradox 新作让新手也能上手

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

Paradox 的新作《Afterworld》是一款后末日题材的宏大战略游戏，其设计重点在于降低新玩家的上手门槛。Eurogamer 的 Matt Wales 在试玩后表示，这款游戏可能成为他第一款真正能玩明白的 Paradox 宏大战略游戏。游戏通过一系列明智的设计决策，避免了该类型常见的复杂性和信息过载问题，使新手也能较快掌握核心玩法。目前游戏尚未公布正式发售日期，但已引发玩家对 Paradox 新方向的关注。

**「背景」** Paradox Interactive 以制作深度复杂的宏大战略游戏闻名，如《十字军之王》和《钢铁雄心》系列，这些游戏以历史模拟和精细管理著称，但常因学习曲线陡峭而让新手望而却步。《Afterworld》是 Paradox 首次尝试后末日题材的宏大战略游戏，旨在通过简化机制和更直观的界面吸引更广泛的玩家群体。

**「影响」** 如果《Afterworld》能成功平衡深度与易用性，它可能吸引大量因复杂度过高而放弃 Paradox 游戏的玩家，扩大宏大战略游戏的受众基础，并可能影响 Paradox 未来作品的设计方向。

## 原文 excerpt

Paradox's Afterworld feels like the first grand strategy game I can properly wrap my head around
The new, clear option.
I've got a bit of a funny relationship with Paradox's grand strategy games. I love the idea of them, of waging and flirting my way across history on the most granular of levels. But despite my enthusiasm, and despite my best efforts, I've never properly cracked one: it doesn't take long before I'm feeling thoroughly overwhelmed and my attention quickly begins to wane. I bring this up, because my relatively brief hands-on time with Paradox's new post-apocalyptic grand strategy game Afterworld suggests - thanks to some smart design decisions - it might finally be the one for me.
Like Paradox's other in-house grand strategy games, Afterworld brings its own unique twist. So while Crusader Kings is very much the studio's take on role-playing, Afterworld draws inspiration from survival and settlement building games, letting players loose in a post-apocalyptic version of North America blighted by hazardous terrain and dangerous old-world ruins. Here, they're the guiding hand for a ragtag group of survivors, who they'll grow from humble beginnings into a continent-spanning empire - perhaps by utilising ancient technology and inspiring fear, or perhaps by building a civilisation that takes care of its people.
And it all starts with a choice: who will your survivors be? In the build I played, Afterworld features three starting origins, each with its own narrative flavour (Paradox describes Afterworld as "story rich", and most objectives, choice points, and discoveries come with art, story scraps, and flavour text, meaning that while you're not role-playing in the same ways as Crusader Kings, there's still the sense that you're part of your own epic adventure). Origins, more specifically, are intended to help you lean into certain playstyles faster.
