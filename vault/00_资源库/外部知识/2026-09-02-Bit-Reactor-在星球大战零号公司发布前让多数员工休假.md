# Bit Reactor 在《星球大战：零号公司》发布前让多数员工休假

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

据多家游戏媒体报道，Bit Reactor 工作室在《星球大战：零号公司》发布前数周让“多数”参与开发的员工休假，该游戏已于上周正式发售。此次休假与工作室创始人之间的法律纠纷有关，该纠纷使 Bit Reactor 的所有权归属成为疑问。受影响的员工在游戏发布后仍处于不确定状态，可能面临无薪处境。这一事件凸显了游戏行业在重大项目发布前后的不稳定性。

**「背景」** Bit Reactor 是一家游戏开发工作室，由 Alison Kelly 和 Greg Foertsch 共同创立，两人曾于 2022 年游戏开发者大会上同台亮相。该工作室开发的《星球大战：零号公司》于上周发布，并获得好评。然而，在游戏发布前，工作室联合创始人之间因所有权问题爆发法律纠纷，导致大部分开发人员被暂时停职。据 Game File 报道，工作室发言人表示希望这些被停职的开发人员能恢复工作并补发工资，但联合创始人 Alison Kelly 在诉讼中透露，这些停职事件原定于 6 月开庭审理。

**「影响」** 对于 Bit Reactor 的员工而言，此次休假意味着在游戏发布的关键时期失去工作和收入保障，而所有权纠纷可能进一步影响他们的雇佣状态和未来补偿。

Bit Reactor furloughed "many of its workers" in the weeks before Star Wars Zero Company's launch
Sources claim 80% of the developer's staff were furloughed, and that a skeleton crew is left to address post-launch bugs
Star Wars Zero Company developer Bit Reactor furloughed "many of its workers" prior to the game's release.
A representative for Bit Reactor told Game File it was a "difficult decision" made "before it was clear how the launch would go." They also clarified that it was not influenced by publisher Electronic Arts or Star Wars license holder Disney.
Zero Company has been positively received by players and critics, and is currently ranked second on Steam's top sellers list.
Sources told Game File that 80% of Bit Reactor's staff were furloughed, leaving a skeleton crew to address any post-launch bugs for Zero Company.
The decision was reportedly due to Bit Reactor having "run out of most of its money just ahead of the launch of its first game."
Former senior technical artist Zachariah Scott claimed on LinkedIn that the studio was "concealing the fact that the team was furloughed weeks ago."
Scott also told Game File that budgets were "tight" during his tenure and that studio founder Greg Foertsch "often mentioned hoping to secure a new game deal to keep things going."
Scott recalled that developers wanted more lightsaber animations but were told there was not enough budget. He described the project as developed with "shocking austerity."
Bit Reactor's representative said the studio "hoped to bring furloughed workers back, suggesting that a successful launch of the game could be impactful."
However, Scott noted that staff he spoke with "were less confident that even if there were returns, that all who were furloughed would get their old jobs again."
The furloughs were also referenced in a 2024 lawsuit filed by former chief operating officer Alison Kelly, which noted the studio also "terminated contractors."
