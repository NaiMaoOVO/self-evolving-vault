# 《赛博朋克：边缘行者 2》定档 10 月 20 日，预告片暗示复仇与救赎

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

《赛博朋克：边缘行者 2》正式宣布将于 10 月 20 日在 Netflix 首播，并发布了最新预告片，暗示故事将围绕“复仇与救赎”展开。该系列是 CD Projekt Red 与 Netflix 基于《赛博朋克 2077》世界观合作推出的动画续作，此前已公布四位新的夜之城主角。预告片进一步渲染了剧情基调，但具体情节细节尚未完全披露。

**「背景」** 《赛博朋克：边缘行者》是 2022 年首播的动画剧集，设定在《赛博朋克 2077》的夜之城，讲述街头少年大卫·马丁内斯的故事。该剧获得广泛好评，并推动了游戏的热度回升。第二季将延续这一世界观，聚焦新的角色和故事线。

**「影响」** 对于《赛博朋克 2077》的粉丝和动画爱好者而言，这一消息确认了续作的回归时间，并可能再次带动游戏及相关内容的关注度。

## 原文 excerpt

"The lines between redemption and revenge will blur", promises Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2's latest trailer as the series locks in a late October release
Edgerunning closer
Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2, the second series of the anime CD Projekt Red and Netflix grafted onto the former's cyberverse following Cyberpunk 2077's release, will premiere on October 20th. Having previously revealed the four new Night City dwellers its plot will be following, the show's creators have now put out another trailer teasing the tale of "revenge and redemption" in store.
The thing that's stuck out to me about the new teaser, which you can watch above, is that it appears to split the four leads off into two pairs. Veteran edgerunner Weak Kingsley, the bloke in the grey coat, looks to have been tasked with helping chromed-up Talia Yang escape the corpo life before it chews her up and spits her out, all while confronting his own past demons as he adjusts to life without cyberware. Meanwhile, nomad netrunner D's escaped a fire that wiped out his clan and ends up meeting Roman Carax, the kid with the camcorder keen to document the city's stories.
From there, there's a lot of shooting and shouting that all four look to have a hand in and some creepy chat about D being "a weapon made out of people". All in all, the series looks like a shot of action and arguably over-the-top emotional drama right to your frontal lobe. I'm keen to give it more of a chance to win me over than I did the first series, especially now I know it'll be coming around the time most games aside from Call of Duty run for the hills to avoid doing anything major around GTA 6's console launch. I love Cyberpunk as a game, though a lot of that comes from being able to explore Night City myself, but I think I could be persuaded to dig the anime with a bit more exposure.
