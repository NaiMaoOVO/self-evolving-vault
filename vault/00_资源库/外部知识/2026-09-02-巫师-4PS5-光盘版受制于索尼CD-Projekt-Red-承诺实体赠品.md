# 《巫师 4》PS5 光盘版受制于索尼，CD Projekt Red 承诺实体赠品

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

CD Projekt Red 表示，无法保证《巫师 4》在 2028 年发售时会有 PlayStation 5 光盘版，因为是否生产光盘取决于索尼的制造工厂。联合首席执行官 Michał Nowakowski 承认，公司对此无能为力。尽管如此，CD Projekt Red 承诺无论光盘版是否推出，都会提供包含实体赠品的游戏包装盒。这一消息反映了实体游戏介质在主机平台上的供应日益受限的趋势。

**「背景」** 近年来，游戏行业逐渐转向数字发行，索尼等主机厂商在实体光盘的生产和分销上拥有决定权。CD Projekt Red 作为游戏开发商，无法控制索尼的生产决策，因此光盘版的可用性取决于平台方的制造安排。

**「影响」** 对于期待购买《巫师 4》PS5 光盘版的玩家，这可能意味着他们需要依赖数字版或等待后续的光盘生产决定，而实体收藏者仍可获得包含实体赠品的包装盒。

## 原文 excerpt

CD Projekt Red powerless regarding PS5 disc edition of The Witcher 4, but promises a game box with "feelies" whatever happens
"We have no influence on the policies of the first parties."
CD Projekt Red, as influential as it is, has said it cannot do anything to guarantee a PlayStation 5 disc-based edition of The Witcher 4 when the game is released in 2028. It is, joint joint-CEO Michał Nowakowski said, at the mercy of Sony's manufacturing factories.
"We don't know what's going to be the case," Nowakowski told The Game Business at Gamescom last week. "We know what PlayStation has announced. And we have no influence on the policies of the first parties. The discs for the first parties, including PlayStation, have to be manufactured in their own factories. So, if they stop manufacturing indeed by 2028, then yes [we won't have a disc]."
Sony's current and controversial plan is to stop disc manufacturing starting January 2028, but whether this will be a hard cut-off date or a softer, more rolling discontinuation remains to be seen. Nor do we know exactly when in 2028 The Witcher 4 will be released.
For reference, The Witcher 3 arrived in May 2015 (though it originally targeted an autumn 2014 release before being delayed a couple of times), whereas Cyberpunk 2077 arrived in December 2020 (after missing an April release date and then a September release date and then a November release date). I expect, given The Witcher 4's pulling power, CD Projekt Red is aiming for the blockbuster-favouring autumn. Its chances of dodging the PS5 disc discontinuation cut-off, then, are slim.

Nevertheless, CD Projekt Red has pledged to make a physical edition of The Witcher 4 however it can. "We're big fans of people having access to tangible collectible things," Nowakowski said.
