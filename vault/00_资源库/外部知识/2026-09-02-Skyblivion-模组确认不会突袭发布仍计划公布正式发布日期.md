# Skyblivion 模组确认不会突袭发布，仍计划公布正式发布日期

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

Skyblivion 模组团队确认，该大型《上古卷轴 4：湮没》重制模组不会效仿 Bethesda 的《湮没重制版》进行突袭发布（shadow drop）。相反，他们计划在准备好后公布新的发布日期，此前该模组曾计划于 2025 年底发布，但已推迟至 2026 年。这一决定是基于团队十多年的辛勤工作，他们希望以常规方式发布，而不是突然上线。目前具体发布日期尚未确定，但团队表示会适时公布。

**「背景」** Skyblivion 是一个由志愿者团队开发的模组，旨在使用《上古卷轴 5：天际》的引擎完整重制《上古卷轴 4：湮没》。该项目已开发超过十年，原定于 2025 年发布，但已推迟至 2026 年。Bethesda 官方在 2025 年突袭发布了《湮没重制版》，引发了社区对 Skyblivion 是否会效仿的猜测。

**「影响」** 对于期待 Skyblivion 的玩家和模组社区，这一确认意味着他们需要继续等待，但可以期待一个正式的发布日期公布，而不是突然上线。这有助于管理社区预期，并确保模组在发布时达到团队预期的质量。

## 原文 excerpt

Skyblivion "will not be shadow dropping after over a decade of hard work", say modders still planning a regular release date reveal despite delays
The unofficial Oblivion remake project was originally scheduled to debut last year
Skyblivion won't be following the lead of Bethesda's Oblivion Remastered with a sudden shadow drop of its own, the folks behind the massive Oblivion remake mod have confirmed. Instead, they've plans to reveal a new release date for the mod, which was previously being aimed to arrive late last year, when they're ready.
Responding to some recent fan speculation in Skyblivion's Discord server about a shadow drop being a possibility for the mod, since its creators only have themselves to answer to rather than any publishers, the mod's communications lead Evan Flatt ruled out following the same path as the official revamp of The Elder Scrolls 4. "We will not be shadow dropping after over a decade of hard work," he wrote. "Don't worry, once we're ready to give everyone a date we will make sure everyone knows it!"
As much as the modders might have the freedom to release how and when they like, I think setting a date and thus having a chance to build up to release is a shrewd move. Mods can't rely on the natural x factor of being something from the game's original studio or devs, as Oblivion Remastered could, so taking time to get folks who might not have been actively keeping tabs on Skyblivion on board is likely for the best. Especially given the delays and public disagreements the project's suffered, which I'd say have made it even more worth emphasising that it is finally done and on the way, rather than relying on the one-time shock of a release without warning to draw in eyeballs.
