# 《瘟疫传说》新作提前一周遭泄露

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

据 Eurogamer 报道，新作《Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy》在正式发售前一周疑似已被破解并在网上传播，可能导致剧透内容提前出现。该游戏是《瘟疫传说》系列的最新作品，此次泄露事件可能影响玩家的游戏体验，并引发对游戏安全性的担忧。目前官方尚未对此事发表正式声明，建议玩家在发售前谨慎浏览相关社区和媒体内容以避免剧透。

**「背景」** 《瘟疫传说》系列是由 Asobo Studio 开发、Focus Entertainment 发行的动作冒险游戏，以其黑暗的中世纪背景和鼠群机制著称。系列此前作品如《瘟疫传说：无罪》和《瘟疫传说：安魂曲》均获得好评，因此新作备受期待。游戏在发售前被破解并泄露并非罕见，但提前一周泄露可能对发行商造成损失，并影响玩家体验。

**「影响」** 对于期待《Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy》的玩家，此次泄露可能导致游戏剧情和关键内容被剧透，削弱发售时的惊喜感；同时，发行商可能面临销量损失和声誉风险。

Careful, the new Plague Tale game appears to have leaked a week ahead of launch
Rats!
Plague Tale fans beware: the new game, Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy, appears to have been cracked and shared online a week before release. It means spoilers may start appearing online, so be careful what you watch and read.
News of Resonance's pirated PC version originally spread on the Plague Tale subreddit, but moderators there are working hard to stamp out any information, screenshots, or videos shared from it. "No more temp bans," the moderators said, with their best stern faces. "Any more posts on the leaks/asking for info about the game via the leaks will result in a permanent ban. If it continues, the sub will be locked down for a week." I've contacted Asobo for comment.
Watch on YouTubeResonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is due for release 27th August - next Thursday - and marks a notable departure for the series in that it has a new main character and introduces new core gameplay. Gone are Amicia and Hugo, the sister and brother duo who led the previous two games, and in their place is Sophia, who we met in A Plague Tale: Requiem.
This is the story of Sophia's connection to the Macula, that spooky rat plague, in events that occurred 15 years before the original Plague Tale game - before Hugo and maybe even Amicia were born. Although it's also about the story of Theseus and the Minotaur, whose timeline we explore parallel to hers.
Gone, also, is the core stealth gameplay of the series in favour of more upfront combat, as Sophia takes the fight to her enemies, weaving around them with a variety of different blades and their associated moves that she can collect, and roping in enemies from afar. Combat works well, as I discovered after playing a chunk of Resonance: A Plague Tale earlier this year. And that's on top of pleasantly taxing light-related puzzles, and of course the cinematic splendour the series is known for.
