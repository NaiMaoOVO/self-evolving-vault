# Take-Two 再次申请 Discord 传票追查 GTA 6 泄密者

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

Take-Two 互动软件公司（Take-Two Interactive）在追查《侠盗猎车手 6》（GTA 6）泄密者的法律行动中再次升级，向法院申请了另一份针对 Discord 的传票，但这次要求法院将传票细节保密。此前，Rockstar 母公司已因两周前发生的大量 GTA 6 泄露事件而采取法律行动。此次传票特别指定了相关个人，但具体内容因密封令而未被公开。这一举动表明 Take-Two 正加大力度追查泄密源头，并可能涉及更广泛的法律调查。

**「背景」** 《侠盗猎车手 6》是 Rockstar Games 备受期待的游戏，其开发过程一直受到严格保密。近期，大量游戏内画面和细节被泄露到网上，引发了广泛关注。Take-Two 作为 Rockstar 的母公司，此前已对泄密者采取法律行动，包括向 Discord 等平台发出传票，以获取用户信息。此次新的密封传票是这一法律行动的延续，旨在进一步追查泄密者身份。

**「影响」** 对于参与或知晓 GTA 6 泄密事件的个人，此次密封传票可能意味着他们面临更直接的法律风险，因为 Take-Two 正通过法律手段获取 Discord 用户信息。对于游戏行业而言，这显示了发行商对保护知识产权的强硬态度，可能对其他潜在泄密者起到威慑作用。

As the legal hunt for the GTA 6 leaker continues, Take-Two reportedly tries another more secretive Discord subpoena, this time specifying individuals
"It now seeks more targeted information."
Rockstar parent company Take-Two has escalated its hunt for the people behind the numerous Grand Theft Auto 6 leaks from two weeks ago. The publisher has filed a request for another subpoena, though this time it's asking the court to withhold the details from the public and keep it sealed.
The subpoena request, as reported by TorrentFreak, is the second one aimed at Discord. Take-Two previously filed subpoena requests targeting multiple companies whose products were used to store and disseminate the leaked GTA 6 footage, including Discord, Microsoft, and X. Following these filings, Discord said it had yet to be served, and that it would "evaluate the validity and scope before responding" if and when it was.
Take-Two's tactics seem to be changing a bit, as the company is now trying to keep the public from being able to see details of the subpoena. Unlike the original public subpoenas, no one outside the court will be able to learn details of the information Take-Two is seeking this time. The company was criticised for the scope and scale of the information it requested in Discord's initial subpoena, which targeted hundreds of thousands of users across several Discord servers where the GTA 6 leaks mainly circulated.
The new filing, made on 28th August, appears to have a more specific target in mind. "Take-Two has identified one additional Discord user and obtained additional identifying information for a previously identified Discord user, as well as additional information concerning the community services identified in its prior subpoena for which it now seeks more targeted information," it reads.
