# Xbox 主机广告或利好游戏业

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

广告技术分析平台 Happydemics 的首席营销官 Virginie Chesnais 发表观点文章，认为 Xbox 在主机上投放广告可能对游戏行业有利。她指出，这类广告能为游戏开发者提供新的收入来源，并可能降低游戏价格或支持免费游戏模式。文章强调，只要广告实施得当、不干扰游戏体验，就能在商业变现和用户体验之间取得平衡。该观点代表行业内部对主机广告的积极看法，但尚未有具体数据或案例支持。

**「背景」** 主机游戏平台传统上依赖游戏销售和订阅服务盈利，而广告在主机生态中较为少见。近年来，随着游戏开发成本上升和免费游戏模式普及，开发者寻求多元化收入，广告成为潜在选项。Xbox 作为主要游戏平台，其广告策略可能影响行业趋势。

**「影响」** 如果 Xbox 的广告策略得以实施，可能为游戏开发者提供额外收入来源，进而影响游戏定价或商业模式，但具体影响取决于广告形式和用户接受度。

Why Xbox's console ads could be a good thing for gaming | Opinion
The increasing price of games and consoles is creating a barrier for the everyday gamer – advertising can help lower the cost of access
Virginie Chesnais is CMO at the ad tech analytics platform Happydemics.
Gamers have long been wary of in-game adverts, and not without reason. Ads in gaming have a mixed reputation, with redirects to different websites or apps, deceptive content, or impossible-to-find close buttons all being common frustrations.
A recent eMarketers study found that 52% of gamers would quit playing if they encountered multiple disruptive ad features. Losing half your audience at the point of engaging them isn't a great strategy.
But ads don't have to be bad. Indeed, the same survey claimed that one in five gamers would play a title more frequently if in-game ads were of high quality.
So, with Xbox currently testing out its new ad-supported game streaming service and the cost of playing steadily increasing, high-quality ads may well find a happy home in gaming.
Why ads? Why now?
The case for providing some form of ads-for-games-type deal is more attractive than some may think, having been at least partially proven by linear media. As Netflix, Disney, and other streaming services have shown, there is money to be made by providing a consistent service while also offering ad-supported tiers. If Xbox's approach proves successful, it will not only retain paid customers through its subscription service, but it will also unlock a brand new, previously untapped revenue stream at a time when the brand sorely needs it.
"Ad-supported tiers can open up a new way for players to enter the gaming sector without having to fork out for large upfront purchases"
This matters because console gaming is becoming much more of a premium service. The prices of games, consoles, accessories and downloadable content are becoming harder and harder for people to justify, creating a barrier for the everyday gamer.
