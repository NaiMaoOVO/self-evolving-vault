# UKIE 携手 Discord 推出 Go To Market Academy 支持英国独立游戏工作室

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

英国互动娱乐协会（UKIE）与 Discord 及 GameFlex 合作，推出了面向英国早期独立游戏工作室的 Go To Market Academy 商业计划。该计划旨在帮助这些工作室掌握市场推广和商业化技能，以应对游戏行业中的商业挑战。Discord 的参与将为参与者提供社区建设和玩家互动方面的支持。这一举措反映了游戏行业对独立开发者商业能力培养的日益重视。

**「背景」** UKIE 是英国游戏行业的贸易机构，长期为本土游戏企业提供支持。独立游戏工作室通常缺乏市场推广和商业运营的资源，而 Discord 作为游戏社区平台，能够提供与玩家直接互动的渠道。此次合作旨在弥补独立开发者在商业技能上的不足。

**「影响」** 该计划将为英国早期独立游戏工作室提供实用的市场推广和商业化指导，可能提升其产品发布成功率。具体效果尚待观察，但 Discord 的参与可能增强参与者在社区运营方面的能力。

## 原文 excerpt

UKIE launches Discord-backed Go To Market Academy, a business program to support early-stage indie devs
Program aims to help UK-based studios build go-to-market strategies and sustainable audiences
UKIE has partnered with Discord and GameFlex to launch the Go To Market Academy, a business program to support UK-based indie studios.
The Academy aims to provide early-stage developers with the tools to build go-to-market strategies and sustainable communities.
The program consists of six online modules covering diagnostics and insights, marketing strategy and campaign execution, using Discord for community engagement, and studio branding. Industry guest speakers will also participate.
Open to 10 to 12 studios, the program begins with an in-person session at EGX at MCM Comic Con in London next month. Online sessions will follow throughout November and early December.
Sessions will conclude with another in-person event, where participating studios will present their go-to-market plans developed through "a shared case study over the course of the programme."
Interested studios can apply here, with applications closing on September 30, 2026.
"Go-to-market planning is where a lot of promising games fall down, not because studios lack good ideas, but because they haven't had the time or support to build the audience and community that gives those ideas a chance to flourish," said UKIE CEO Nick Poole.
"The Go To Market Academy gives indie studios the space to work through that properly, with direct input from Discord on a platform that’s become central to how games communities actually form and grow."
Discord director of public policy EMEA Mauro Sanna added: "The UK's indie games scene is full of talent and opportunity, and we're delighted to partner with UKIE to help it thrive. We're excited to help this group of UK developers learn how to make the most of Discord to connect with our highly engaged gaming audience, build communities and grow their games."
