# Nvidia DLSS 5 本周随 NBA 2K27 发布，AI 美化滤镜引发争议

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

Nvidia 的 DLSS 5 神经渲染技术将于本周随《NBA 2K27》正式发布，此前该技术已被模组制作者从游戏文件中提前挖掘出来。DLSS 5 引入了一个 AI 滤镜，在年初公布时曾因给游戏角色进行生成式 AI 美化而受到批评。该滤镜被戏称为“yassification”，会改变角色外观，使其更像社交媒体上的模特。此次发布标志着 DLSS 5 首次正式亮相，但技术细节有限，主要关注点在于其争议性的视觉效果。

**「背景」** DLSS（深度学习超级采样）是英伟达的一项 AI 渲染技术，通过神经网络提升游戏画质和帧率。DLSS 5 是该系列的最新版本，引入了神经渲染功能，但因其 AI 滤镜会显著改变游戏角色外观而受到批评，例如将《生化危机：安魂曲》主角 Grace Ashcroft“美化”成类似 Instagram 模特的样子。该技术原计划于今年晚些时候发布，但上周被模组制作者从《NBA 2K27》的游戏文件中提前挖掘出来。

**「影响」** 对于《NBA 2K27》玩家和 Nvidia 显卡用户，DLSS 5 的发布意味着他们将在游戏中体验到新的 AI 渲染功能，但该滤镜可能改变角色外观，引发玩家不满。

After being prematurely dug out of game files by modders late last week, Nvidia's DLSS 5 neural rendering tech is making its official debut in a matter of days. "While previous DLSS technologies focused on reconstructing existing scene data to deliver higher-resolution images, additional frames, or cleaner ray-traced lighting," Nvidia wrote in a post announcing DLSS 5's release on September 3rd. "DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering takes the next step by using AI to generate the rich lighting and material details that real-time performance constraints once forced developers to strip away, allowing them to realise their original artistic vision.
"And instead of generating frames from text prompts, 3D-Guided Neural Rendering uses the game engine’s rendered frame, complete with its artist-designed geometry, textures, and lighting buffers, as an unyielding foundation. DLSS 5 becomes the final rendering stage of the graphics pipeline, infusing scenes with lifelike lighting and materials while ensuring the engine frame defines what must remain, and the artist directs what may change."
Yep, Nvidia continue to insist that the tech "preserves artistic intent" even as it morphs faces, by maintaining the "underlying facial proportions and geometry" devs have designed and only messing with the likes of "light responses and material detail".
