# 索尼律师称理性消费者已知不拥有数字游戏

## AI 摘要（Horizon 日报）

索尼的律师在法庭上辩称，理性的消费者已经知道他们并不真正拥有数字游戏，这一论点出现在 PlayStation 逐步停止生产实体光盘的背景下。该法律论据旨在应对消费者对数字购买所有权的质疑，强调购买数字游戏仅获得使用许可而非所有权。索尼正计划减少甚至取消实体光盘的生产，推动全面数字化，这引发了关于消费者权益和长期访问权的讨论。目前，这一法律立场尚未得到法院最终裁决，但其结果可能影响游戏行业数字销售模式的透明度。

**「背景」** 传统上，购买实体游戏光盘意味着拥有物理副本，可以转售、出借或永久保留。然而，数字游戏购买通常受最终用户许可协议（EULA）约束，消费者仅获得有限使用权，而非所有权。随着游戏行业向数字化分发转型，索尼等公司面临消费者对数字购买长期可访问性的质疑，特别是在服务器关闭或服务终止的情况下。

**「影响」** 如果法院采纳索尼的论点，可能进一步巩固游戏公司对数字内容分发的控制权，削弱消费者对数字游戏所有权的期望，并影响未来数字销售条款的透明度。

As PlayStation's plan to scrap the production of game discs inches closer, Sony's lawyers are arguing that "reasonable consumers" already know that they don't own digital games.
This comes in the middle of a class action lawsuit filed against Sony by four customers, who stated back in July that the PlayStation Store's disclosures don't do enough to comply with a certain 2025 California law around digital ownership. Companies are required to let customers know loud and clear that they're not owed access to a digital product forever, even if they've already paid for it, and the claimants feel Sony's not being obvious enough about how digital purchases don't mean digital ownership.
For context, the PlayStation Store does link to both the company's Terms Of Service and Software Product Licensing Agreement when you go to buy a digital game on the storefront. The part that states that "virtual content is licensed, not owned" comes several hundred words into the documents, however.
As reported by The Game File, Sony's legal team fired back in a filing from August 21. Sony said most "reasonable consumers would not be misled" by the company's disclosures.
